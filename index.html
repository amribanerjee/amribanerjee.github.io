About Me

I am an incoming undergraduate researcher at the Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences, University of Massachusetts Amherst. My research focuses on AI evaluation, collaborative language models, biomedical AI, and graph machine learning. I build evaluation frameworks and scalable machine learning architectures to quantify and guarantee the reliability of edge-deployed models in high-stakes environments.

Currently, I am a Research Intern at the BioNLP Lab (UMass Amherst) and the eBrain Lab (NYU Abu Dhabi), where I engineer hybrid-operator synthesis pipelines to algorithmically mitigate artifacts in 3D Computed Tomography scans.

Research Interests

  • AI Evaluation & Reliability: Behavioral consistency, capability mapping, multi-agent frameworks
  • Biomedical AI: Medical image processing, interpretable volume refinement, clinical LLMs
  • Graph Machine Learning: Optimal transport, spectral graph theory, topological stability
