Amritesh Banerjee

Undergraduate AI Researcher at the Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences, University of Massachusetts Amherst.

My research investigates the structural integrity of autonomous systems. I focus on AI evaluation, collaborative language models, clinical graph machine learning, and biomedical image refinement. I build scalable machine learning architectures to quantify and guarantee the reliability of edge-deployed models in high-stakes environments.
